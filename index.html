JPC Systems Ltd

Independent software consultancy. Tunbridge Wells, England.

JPC Systems Ltd designs and builds complex distributed systems, with a particular focus on work where low latency and correctness matter: algorithmic trading systems, matching engines and real-time data platforms.

The company works with businesses that need senior engineering capability for a defined piece of work, either building the system itself or advising the team that will.

Services

Software engineering
Design, implementation and integration of business-critical systems, from low latency services and matching engines through to data pipelines and internal tooling.
Cloud and infrastructure
Platform design, automation and day-to-day operations across cloud providers, container orchestration and infrastructure as code.
Technical consultancy
Architecture review and independent advice on latency, throughput, reliability, security and scale.

How engagements work

Work is carried out on a business-to-business basis under a contract for services, normally for a defined scope or a fixed term. The company provides its own equipment, is responsible for the quality of what it delivers, and will correct any defects in that work at its own cost.
